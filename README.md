## 📖 How to Use BC-ModelVault

### First Time Opening

When you first open BC-ModelVault, you'll see a welcome screen. Choose where you want your models to be stored. A folder like `C:\AI-Models` works great.

### Downloading a Model

1. Click the **Download** tab at the top
2. Type what you're looking for in the search box
3. Browse the results
4. Click **Download** on any model you want
5. Watch the progress bar fill up

You'll see a notification when your download is complete.

### Checking Your Models

The **Library** tab shows everything you've downloaded. Each model has a green checkmark if it's healthy.

### Organizing with Folders

Right-click any model in your library to move it to a new folder. Create folders like "Portraits," "Anime," or "Text Models" to keep things tidy.

## 🚨 Troubleshooting Common Problems

### Download is slow
Check your internet connection. Large AI models can take several hours to download.

### Download fails halfway
Don't worry. BC-ModelVault lets you resume failed downloads. Just click download again, and it will pick up where it left off.

### Can't find a model
Try a different search term. Model names can vary between creators.

### App won't open
Right-click the app icon and select "Run as administrator." If that doesn't work, restart your computer.

### Out of space
Delete old models you no longer need. BC-ModelVault makes it easy to remove files.
